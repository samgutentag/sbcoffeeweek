Methodology, Honestly

Counts come from a privacy-light tracker: no cookies, no user IDs, no precise location. Numbers are actions, not unique people (someone who opened a restaurant twice counts twice). This report is built from the final archived snapshot rather than live analytics, so figures are the end-of-event totals; if analytics sampling occurred during peak traffic they may run slightly low. Audience breakdowns (devices, referrers) require data captured during the event window and are omitted where unavailable. Beacon-based tracking loses a small number of events by design, and like counts allowed un-liking.
